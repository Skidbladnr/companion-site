Privacy policy

This policy describes ATHENA Companion, a private macOS application maintained by its developer for a small invited group. For privacy questions or assistance, contact skidbladniir@gmail.com.

Information you choose to connect

Google sign-in provides account identity, including your email address and basic profile information. With your authorization, Companion reads selected Gmail account content, including message text, headers and thread context; lists calendars; and reads events from calendars you select. A bounded recent-mail and calendar cache supports briefings and follow-up work. Additional thread context or supported attachments may be read when the workflow calls for them.

If you connect the optional Google Drive integration, Companion can read file and folder metadata and authorized file content for retrieval and project work. Any separately authorized file-filing workflow is limited to the files and destination approved through that workflow. Gmail and Calendar connections do not authorize Drive access.

How the application uses this information

Connected information supports the features you request: viewing correspondence and schedules, source-linked briefings, questions about selected material, and preparing drafts. Source content is not permission to act. The Gmail and Calendar integrations do not send mail, edit messages, or change calendar events.

Your preferences, saved readings, conversations and drafts provide continuity when you return to Companion. They may include excerpts from your connected sources.

Local storage and credentials

The application uses macOS Keychain for connection credentials and a Keychain-backed encrypted local workspace for supported retained content. Credentials are not supplied to language models. Copies you explicitly export, and any backups you create, have their own storage and deletion lifecycle.

Optional processing by OpenAI

If you enable OpenAI processing, Companion sends the selected source material needed for a request, your question, relevant conversation context and applicable preferences to the OpenAI API. This can include Google message or event content. OpenAI processes that material to provide briefings and responses under the applicable API terms and data controls. Cloud processing is not a promise that all information remains on your Mac. Review your processing choices before using confidential material.

Companion does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. ATHENA Companion’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Connection caches are bounded by the relevant feature’s time and size limits. Saved readings, conversations and owner-authored drafts may remain until replaced or removed through the application, or until the workspace is deleted. Disconnecting a source revokes its local authority and clears its connection cache and credentials; it is not a promise to erase previously exported files, separately retained drafts or backups.

You may also revoke Companion’s Google access in your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically erase copies already saved locally. Contact the developer if you need help removing the local workspace. You do not need to send message contents or credentials to request help.

This website and support

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no sign-in form, advertising scripts or analytics added by Companion. GitHub may process ordinary hosting information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its privacy statement. Visiting this site does not give it access to your connected Google accounts. If you contact the developer, the information you send is used to answer your request.

Policy changes

This page will be updated when the application’s data practices change. Any materially different source permission or processing destination requires the corresponding application setup and authorization.
